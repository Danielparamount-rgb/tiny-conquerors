If someone drops

The match doesn't end. Every machine hands the empty seat to the computer on the same agreed turn, and the town keeps building. When they come back with the same code, they get it straight back — army, buildings, wherever the AI left them.

Drop detected in 10.1 s

Free relay, honest about it

The server sleeps after 15 minutes idle. The first host press after a quiet spell waits ~50s while it wakes — the game says so rather than pretending.

Tuned for the phone in your hand

128 × 128

Larger boards give more room to expand. 128 is the ceiling — above it phone browsers refuse the canvas.

Victory conditions

Siege and gunpowder

Scorpions punch through a line of men. Trebuchets take three seconds to unpack. Bombard cannons wait behind Chemistry.

A real navy

Eight ship types, transports that drown their passengers if there's no shore, and trade cogs that run themselves.

Wildlife worth hunting

Sheep follow whoever finds them. Deer bolt when shot. Boar charge the villager who hit them, and often win.

Three ways to win

Raze every enemy town hall, hold a wonder for five minutes, or gather every relic on the map into your monasteries.

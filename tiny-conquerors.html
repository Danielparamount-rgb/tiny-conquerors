📖  How to play

Touch controls
• Tap a villager or building to select it — commands appear below.
• With units selected, tap berries, trees or gold to gather, tap ground to move, tap an enemy to attack.
• Drag to pan the map, pinch or use +/− to zoom. Tap the minimap to jump.
• Select many: tap the ⬚ button then drag a box (with a mouse, just drag), or double-tap a unit to grab every unit of that type on screen.

Mouse controls — just like the classics: left-click selects anything (units, buildings, even trees, berries and gold mines, to see what's left in them); right-click gives orders — gather, build, attack, garrison, move. Right-click also cancels building placement.

Tips: scout the fog of war with Scout Cavalry, drop resources at Storage Camps near far-off woods and gold, use Spearmen against cavalry and Skirmishers against archers, wall off the fords with Palisades and Gates, smash defenses with Battering Rams, and hold the high ground — units on hills deal +25% damage and take less in return.

New: tap your Town Hall, Tower or Castle with units selected to garrison them inside (each adds arrow damage — ungarrison from the building's panel). Soldiers have an ⚔ Attack-move button: they'll fight their way to wherever you point. Save anytime from the pause menu, and try the 📜 Campaign — now eight missions, ending with a siege train, a war fought entirely by ship, and a Wonder to defend against two warlords at once.

The hunt: the land is stocked. Sheep wander near every town and belong to whoever walks up to them — herd them home with a Scout and slaughter them at your leisure (100 food each, and they cost no population). Deer bolt the moment you shoot at them. Wild Boar are worth 340 food and will gore the hunter who wakes them, so send a crowd, not a man. A slain beast leaves a carcass your villagers butcher like a berry bush. Goths hunt boar hard and carry more meat, Mongols butcher half again as fast, and no one can steal a Celt's flock.

Also new: upgrade to Stone Walls in the Castle Age, trade at the Market, and build a Monastery — its Monks heal your troops and can carry the golden relics scattered around the map back home for a steady gold income. On a keyboard, Ctrl+1–9 sets control groups.

The Conquerors update: all 18 civilizations, each with true bonuses, a unique unit, and a unique technology researched at the Castle. Units now upgrade through their lines as you age — Militia become Champions, Scouts become Hussars. Garrison infantry inside Battering Rams to speed and strengthen them, and blow gates open with the Petard. The Aztecs and Mayans field fleet Eagle Warriors instead of cavalry, and the Huns never build a single house. Raise a Blacksmith to forge attack and armor upgrades through the ages, and research Parthian Tactics at the Archery Range to harden your horse archers. Build a Dock on the riverbank for Fishing Ships, Trade Cogs, Transports, and war Galleys — Vikings launch Longboats and Koreans the armored Turtle Ship.
